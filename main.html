Movie Summary

Interstellar is a science fiction movie about a group of astronauts who travel through a wormhole in search of a new home for humanity as Earth becomes increasingly uninhabitable.

Actors

Matthew McConaughey plays Joseph Cooper, Anne Hathaway plays Amelia Brand, and Jessica Chastain plays Murph. The film also features Michael Caine.
